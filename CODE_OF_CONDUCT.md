# Code of Conduct

DotStories is its community: the people who share dots, help each other and build the project. This code applies
everywhere the community meets: [dotstories.com](https://dotstories.com), comments, this GitHub repository and our
[Discord](https://discord.gg/TJWKQkkuHN).

The full, current version lives at **[dotstories.com/code-of-conduct](https://dotstories.com/code-of-conduct)**.

## Do

- Be kind and welcoming, especially to people who are new.
- Assume positive intent: most mistakes are just mistakes.
- Ask questions, share what you know and help others get their dots working.
- Give credit: say whose dot or idea you built on.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.

## Don't

- Harass, insult, threaten or discriminate against anyone.
- Stalk, dox or publish anyone's private information (doxxing).
- Post sexual content, or anything unlawful or against the [Terms](https://dotstories.com/terms).
- Spam, advertise unasked or flood comments.
- Pass off other people's dots or work as your own.

## Report a problem

Email [info@dotstories.com](mailto:info@dotstories.com) with a link and what happened. We look at every report, keep the
reporter's name private and act on it: a warning, removing content, or suspending or closing an account.
